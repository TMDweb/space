FAQ

Everything you need to know

Can GCC citizens own property in Saudi Arabia?
Yes. Saudi regulations allow GCC citizens to own property with treatment close to that of Saudi citizens, through simple steps. Our team guides you through every procedure until the title deed is issued.
Can I buy without visiting Riyadh?
You can view units through virtual tours and detailed visual materials, and complete most steps remotely. If you prefer a short visit, we arrange it for you with a clear schedule.
What warranties do I get?
A warranty system of up to 25 years covering structural and electrical elements, protecting your asset's value long after handover.
What options are available, and at what prices?
Our portfolio includes villas, apartments, offices, retail, and hotels across several cities. Share your goal and approximate budget, and we will send a list of matching units with current prices.
Who is Makeen?
Makeen is an established Saudi developer since 2010, with more than 100 delivered projects across 7 Saudi cities and warranties of up to 25 years.
